# NZIFSA (New Zealand Ice Figure Skating Association): sources

Hub page: <https://www.nzifsa.org.nz/kiwiskate/>

KiwiSkate is the national learn-to-skate badge programme: Tiny Tots 1 and 2, then Beginner,
Elementary, Basic, Novice 1 and 2 and Advanced, with Figure, Dance and Synchro badges beside
them. Both documents are public on NZIFSA's own site, so they meet the public-material rule
with better provenance than the Learn to Skate USA club copies ever had.

**The `www.` host only.** `nzifsa.org.nz` without it is refused by the session proxy, and the
server answers 406 to a request with no browser user agent.

## What the 2008 document says that the basics pages touch

- Dip: "bend knees to squat position with arms stretched forward. Hips no higher than knees."
- Forward two-foot glide: "length of glide equal to skater's height, feet no further than a
  blade length apart."
- T pushes: "push off from heel to instep on an inside edge".
- Backward skating: alternating "C" sculling, eight consecutive push-glide sequences; later
  "C" pushes, a push and glide on alternating feet.
- Backward one-foot glides "held for skater's length of body".

## Order in the 2025 rules, against the guide's basics

Two-foot turn forward to backward in place is a teaching idea at Beginner and assessed on a
curve at Basic; slalom (Elementary idea); forward pumping round a circle (Elementary);
backward half snowplough (Basic); drag, backward two-foot snowplough and forward pivot with
toe in ice (Novice 1 ideas); backward two-foot turn on a curve (Advanced idea); T-stops
(Advanced). "Teaching Progression Ideas" are taught and not tested (rule 701.2).
