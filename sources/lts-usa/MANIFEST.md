# Learn to Skate USA: sources

Hub pages: <https://www.learntoskateusa.com/basic_skills> · <https://www.learntoskateusa.com/snowplow_sam>

The basics tier is anchored on Learn to Skate USA's Basic 1 to 6 (see `docs/model.md` and
the project note on the basics). Their public pages name the categories and nothing more;
the level-by-level detail is in two documents.

The competition manual's elements event: the elements a skater performs at each level, in
order. It is live on their site and is the citation for the level of every basic it lists.
It gives no mechanics.

## Read but not held: the Basic Skills curriculum

`https://www.learntoskateusa.com/media/1087/curriculum_basicskills.pdf`, the Basic 1 to 6
curriculum with each level's skills and qualifiers ("6–8 in a row", "glide the length of
skater's height", bonus skills). Read on 03/10/2026 through a fetch tool that returned it
from a cache. **The URL returns 404 on the live site the same day**: the site has been
rebuilt and the sitemap (29 URLs) lists no curriculum document. So it cannot be downloaded
and cannot be cited as "on their website" today.

**Taken out on 03/10/2026**, on Martyn's rule that a claim must be verifiable from a document
held here: the backward two-foot glide's "the length of the skater's height", the levels of
the backward snowplough stop, backward stroking and the backward two-foot turn, the pivot and
lunge bonus skills, "both directions" on the hockey stop and the two-foot turn, and "R and L"
where the elements PDF says "either foot". Every Learn to Skate USA level now on a page is
one the elements PDF lists.

Where the two documents disagree the elements PDF wins, as the one held: half-swizzle pumps
are 6–8 consecutive in the curriculum and 4–6 in the elements event, and the page says four to
six.
