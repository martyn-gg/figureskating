# Ice Skating Australia: sources

Hub: <https://isa.org.au> · the learn-to-skate programme lives at <https://aussieskate.au>

Aussie Skate is the national learn-to-skate programme: Tots 1 to 3, Aussie Skate 1 to 10, then
Free Skate levels. The curriculum itself ("Aussie Skate Curriculum", revised by these
communications) is published on aussieskate.au and in the Aussie Skate app.

**Currency.** Communication 125 (2021) renamed the levels and says the curriculum "including
tracking from old to new" is in the Aussie Skate Curriculum 2021 document, which the site does
not link. Communication 186 (2026) changed Free Skate levels only. So cite the manual for how a
skill is done, which no communication has changed, and never for which level it sits at.
