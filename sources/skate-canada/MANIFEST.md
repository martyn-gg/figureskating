## Note on structure

Skate Canada's STAR system runs five streams in parallel — Freeskate, Dance, Skills,
Artistic and Synchro — at STAR 1–10 and Gold. That is a different shape from the BIS
single Skills ladder and from the USFS Moves/Free Skate pair, and the guide should say so
plainly rather than force all three into one table. See `docs/style.md`.
