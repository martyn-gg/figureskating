# sources/

Governing-body material, downloaded by hand and kept out of git.

Everything in here is read, not reproduced. The guide is written **from** these documents
and never **out of** them: our own words, our own diagrams, our own tracings. A syllabus
entry cites its source with `sourceUrl` and stays `verified: { checked: false }` until a
human has confirmed it against the current published version.

`.gitignore` excludes everything under `sources/` except the `MANIFEST.md` files, so the
shopping list is tracked and the documents never are.

## Public material only

Nothing in this guide is built from material that is not publicly accessible. If a
document needs a member login, or is issued to coaches rather than published, it is out of
scope — not because it could not be read, but because a public guide standing on
restricted material would be indefensible the moment anyone asks where it came from.

The plan is to finish the guide from public sources and then approach the governing
bodies. That conversation goes a great deal better if the honest answer to *where did you
get this?* is *your website*. Until then, gaps stay marked as gaps.

## Why this folder exists

PDFs on disk can be read directly. PDFs over the web cannot — fetching returns the
markdown of an HTML page, and a PDF comes back as binary. So the documents come down once,
by hand, and after that the writing is unblocked.

## Layout

    sources/bis/            British Ice Skating
    sources/skate-canada/   Skate Canada
    sources/usfs/           U.S. Figure Skating

Each has a `MANIFEST.md` listing what to download, from where, and why it is wanted. Keep
the original filenames where possible and add the download date to the manifest — these
documents are revised, and a guide written against last year's version is worse than no
guide.

## The one thing to watch

British Ice Skating is updating the National Skills Tests on **01/10/2026**. The change is
**additive** — no exercise is being replaced or removed, and the elements themselves are
unchanged — so writing against the current guidance now is safe, and the October update is
an addition rather than a rewrite. Grab the preview material at the same time as the
current documents and both versions can be ready on the day.
