## What this session did

- **`pushOff` is the side push**, what a beginner learns: knees bent, heels together, the
  right foot turned out 35°, pushed out sideways to full extension, lifted, a rise into the
  glide with the free foot low and close, then bend and bring it back beside the gliding
  heel. Pelvis square throughout; that is the difference a reader should see between the
  two rigs. One `shin.mjs` adjustment on the way (29–30° at the push's first key, fixed by
  a slightly higher hip and the blade a little further forward).
- **`pushOffT` is new: T stroking over two strokes.** The first stroke is Session 29's T
  keys; the second is the first mirrored through `mirrorMove`'s own key map, so the two
  cannot disagree. Path: LFO arc then RFO arc, 35° each at 500 cm, so the top view draws
  the shallow alternating curves Martyn described. Between strokes the free blade is set
  down beside the gliding one, the reference blade hands over on the ice (first on-ice
  handover in the guide; `buildPath`'s displacement rule kept the hip still) and the old
  gliding foot turns out into the next T.
- **The handover has to happen upright.** Each glide leans into its own curve (left blade
  right of the hip, right blade left of it) and the right foot is always right of the left,
  so no single instant satisfies both `lean.mjs` claims. The handover key is on a flat
  (`edge: null`), then 0.01 later onto the new outside edge.
- **`twofoot.mjs` trap:** a move's LAST key cannot introduce a contact. Per-foot fields are
  carried from the key being left, so at t = 1 the final key's `onIce` never appears. The
  second stroke ends on its bend key instead of a set-down.
- **`alsoRig`** in the content schema: a second figure on the same element page.
  Forward stroking mounts `rig: pushOff` and `alsoRig: pushOffT`. `[slug].astro` renders
  both; `rig-names.mjs` reads both as claims.
- **Forward stroking page rewritten**: the side push first, then the T as the progression
  (elegance, power, edges, alternating curves). 159 words against `house.mjs`'s 160.
- **Copy for skaters, not programmers (Martyn):** the "Not checked yet" note no longer says
  the repository is public; the About page no longer says the guide "mirrors for free"; the
  front page's matrix paragraph lost its "stores the three and works the rest out". The
  About page now ends with one line linking the GitHub source.
