## Done

- **Four findings from the 414px review closed.** Chip tap targets to 44px; the
  top-down panel's fixed 880x380 landscape viewBox replaced with 440 units and
  content-driven aspect; `page-shot.mjs` parking every `[data-scrub]` rather than
  `.diagram` only, so light and dark shots land on the same frame.
- **A clipping bug older than the box that revealed it.** Both renderers size from
  the *tracing* and then draw a body around it, so the drawn extent always
  exceeded the measured one. Dead space in the old panel hid it. The fit now
  measures the body from the stored `(t,n)` pose offsets:
  `pad = max(55, reach x BS x 1.15 + 14)`.
- **The limb pair retired.** `--leg-l` / `--leg-r` collapsed to one `--limb`.
  Role is carried by stroke weight (5.4 / 3.0), depth by a casing in the panel's
  own colour, identity by the L and R letters. Opacity removed from every limb in
  all three views.
- **Three checkers.** `framing.mjs` (nothing drawn outside its panel, 1,422
  frame-panels), `ink.mjs` (the skating limb must dominate, per frame, both
  schemes), `contrast.mjs` updated for the retired pair. `--break` fails on all
  three.
- **Docs current.** `state-of-play.md` gained the SIDE-casing caveat and the
  review-method error; `design-brief.md` rewritten where the pair's retirement had
  made it stale — contrast table, the "one hue" rig line, the limb-depth section,
  the verification counts.
- `npm run check` green: seven checkers, 161 pages, 4,010 links.

## Next

   right. The data disagrees: at frame 60 of the waltz jump the skating left foot
   is at n = +17.2 and the free right foot at n = -4.6. Either the poses are
   authored with the opposite handedness, `n` is measured against the curve rather

   that reads `n`, and it blocks item 2.
2. **Set `NEARER.side`** once handedness is known — one character in

3. **The arms.** They take `--limb`, so they carry no left/right distinction, get
   no casing and no depth ordering, and `ink.mjs` does not measure them. A hand
   crossing the torso from behind is the case that shows it. A design session, not
   a repo one — but not before item 1.
4. **Twizzles.** Deliberately skipped since Session 01 and the thing this pass was
   blocking. A travelling multi-rotation turn is not a chain of half turns. Needed
   at Skills 3, 7 and 8.

## Decisions locked

- **One limb colour, not a pair.** The 4.5:1-between-limbs rule existed so colour
  could carry foot identity; identity moved to the letters and the rule outlived
  its job. Amends the Session 01 decision recorded at `design-brief.md` 135-137.
- **One fact per channel.** Weight says which leg bears the skater; a casing says
  which is in front; letters say which foot. No channel carries two facts and none
  may contradict another.
- **Opacity is never a depth or role channel.** It spends contrast and cannot buy
  anything. It was the original defect.
- **Casing follows depth, not role**, enforced by draw order — the nearer limb is
  drawn last and cases over what it crosses, so casing the further limb is
  unrepresentable rather than discouraged.
- **The spiral is the rig review element, not the waltz jump.** Rear-view limb
  overlap: 73 frames of 81 on the spiral, 1 of 70 on the jump.
- **Anything assertable across all elements is a checker, not a screenshot.**

## Open questions

- **Pose handedness** — resolved by a skater reading the rendered poses against
  video, or by finding the authoring convention in the pose data's history.
- **Is 1.8:1 enough weight separation in a rink?** The ink measure ranks the limbs
  correctly, but a proxy for salience is not a model of it. Resolved by a person
  with a phone in a cold rink, not by measurement.
- **Should the airborne state say which foot is about to take the weight?** For 38
  frames of 319 both legs are 3.0 and the panel says "neither", corroborated by the
  dashed tracing. Left deliberately. Resolved by a reader finding it wanting — and
  the answer would be the boot glyph, not a third limb weight.
- **Are the poses right at all?** Standing caveat since Session 01. Everything
  above assumes the geometry; `verified: false` still applies to all of it.

## Prerequisites

- Playwright for the four out-of-band checkers:
  `npm install --no-save playwright && npx playwright install chromium`.
  `check:ink`, `check:framing`, `check:speed`, `check:offline` are outside
  `npm run check` for this reason.
- **`ink.mjs` must run against resolved tokens.** It refuses if `--limb` or
  `--ice` come back empty — a bare page makes every limb measure as a fallback
  colour and the checker then agrees with everything.
- Git is run by the human, not through the device bridge: every command leaves a
  `.lock` behind and `HEAD.lock` blocks writes. Unchanged since Session 01.
