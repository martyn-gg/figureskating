## Then: the mirror and the brackets

Martyn: do the mirror and try the brackets.

- **`mirrorMove`** in `moves.js` reflects a move across the line of travel: feet and hands
  swap, every n and every angle negates, and the path's foot swaps, which flips the lobe and
  with it the curve, the cusp's side and the turn's sense. `rfoThree`, `rfiThree`,
  `rboThree`, `rbiThree` and the four right-foot brackets are one line each.
- **The brackets** are `turnFrom('bracket', ...)`, the three turn's table with the rotation
  reversed. The cusp points out of the circle, looked at and confirmed.
- **The brackets found a fault in the cusp.** The offsets had been integrated in the frame
  of the circle as if it stood still. It turns under the skater, so the contact gripped in
  the rotating frame and not on the ice. `cuspTables` now carries the frame's rotation and
  solves a two-coefficient speed profile per turn. `turnout.mjs` gained a check on the ice
  (4000 steps in ground coordinates, contact along the blade within 0.5°; the old table fails
  it, the new one reads 0.29°), and the drawn-tracing bound went from 3° to 4°, with frames
  where the polyline bends more than 12° a frame counted as unresolved (7 of 364).
- Hashes: 755 frame files of the earlier moves identical; the four three turns and the
  Salchow changed only in frames that draw the cusp (43 files), as the correction should.

## Things to know

- **A one-foot turn is a path segment, not keys.** Put the entry and exit keys at the window's
  ends and wrap the move in `turnMove` so they snap to it. Make their `hipYaw` add to 180 and
  their blade `t` average to zero; then the hip squares up with the blade at the apex and
  `lean.mjs` holds without being told anything.
- `cuspAt` reads `TURNS`, so a bracket would cusp outwards on the same construction. Rockers
  and counters change lobe and are not built.
- The worst blade-along-tracing reading is 2.8° against a bound of 3, on the Salchow's
  twelve-frame turn. That is the polyline being coarse; a faster turn needs more frames.
- Frame hashing: `_to_delete/hash.sh` (untracked). Take the "before" from a `git worktree` of
  HEAD, not the working tree, or edits made while it runs leak into it.
- Fixed at the end (`862921f`): the "also called" line's stray article, and the front page's
  counts, which left out four kinds and claimed every element drew the body.
