## Things to know

- **A one-foot turn is a path segment, not keys.** Put the entry and exit keys at the window's
  ends and wrap the move in `turnMove` so they snap to it. Make their `hipYaw` add to 180 and
  their blade `t` average to zero; then the hip squares up with the blade at the apex and
  `lean.mjs` holds without being told anything.
- `cuspAt` reads `TURNS`, so a bracket would cusp outwards on the same construction. Rockers
  and counters change lobe and are not built.
- The worst blade-along-tracing reading is 2.8° against a bound of 3, on the Salchow's
  twelve-frame turn. That is the polyline being coarse; a faster turn needs more frames.
- Frame hashing: `_to_delete/hash.sh` (untracked). Take the "before" from a `git worktree` of
  HEAD, not the working tree, or edits made while it runs leak into it.
- The template's alias line reads "Also called a Falling and getting up" on a country copy:
  the article is added to every alias and the canonical name is capitalised. Pre-existing,
  not fixed.
