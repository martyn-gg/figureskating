- **Model** (`src/lib/skating.js`): `SECONDS` (jumps taking off where every jump lands:
  the toe loop and the loop, derived from `LANDING`), `comboAt(first, second, count)`
  (name, ISU code such as 2S+2T, rotations; throws if the second does not take off where
  the first lands) and `ALL_COMBOS` (26: every jump into each second at the same count,
  the waltz jump with the singles).
- **Pages**: 26, by `tools/gen-derived.mjs`, kind `combo` (a `combination` is a cluster),
  with a `combo: { first, second, count }` block. Prose from eleven hand-written passages
  (one per first jump, one per second, one for the doubles). New flag
  `--kind=combo` rewrites one kind's files and leaves the rest alone.
- **Section** *Jump combinations* (`elements/in/combo/`) after the jumps: a grid per count,
  first jump against second, cells are the ISU codes. On the Jumps tab.
- **Element page**: chips, three edge tracings (takeoff, the shared RBO, landing), derived
  paragraphs, the two jumps linked, the mirrored version. Jump pages now say which
  combinations they start, and the toe loop and loop say any jump can lead into them.
- **Rigs** (`comboOf` in `src/lib/moves.js`): seven, the four rigged firsts into a loop and
  the three rigged doubles into a double loop. The first jump to its deepest landing key,
  0.9 s on the landing edge with the free leg brought through (two link keys), then the
  second jump from its bend, a whole number of turns round. `buildPath` takes an optional
  `move.frames` (default 320, so nothing else changed); a combination keeps the finer of its
  jumps' frame rates so it turns no further between frames than they do.
- **`tools/jumps.mjs`**: assertions 5 to 7 (pages to `comboAt`, one page per combination,
  rigs to their jumps key for key). `--break=combo` fails 52, `--break=join` fails 132.
- Roadmap item 1 marked done. Jump section note now mentions the doubles.
