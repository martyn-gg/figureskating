## For Martyn to judge

1. **The tracing at a step** is a corner: the old line ends and the new one starts beside it at
   the angle. The weight changes feet at the instant the new blade touches; there is no glide
   on two blades at the angle first (that would be a skid along the old line).
2. **72° and which way.** 64 is the least the rig's hips allow; 72 gives a margin. Every step
   built turns clockwise. For the step forward that is toward the outside of the anticlockwise
   circle, where the body faces in back crossovers; for both mohawks it is forced, since the
   other way puts both feet turned in.
3. **The inside mohawk's feet**: the left heel goes down 8 cm behind and 8 cm inside the right
   blade's middle, and its line crosses the old one heading out.
4. **The Lutz route as read**: "a right forward mohawk" taken as a forward stroke from LFO onto
   RFO, then RFO→LBO. The left blade goes down ahead of the right toe (beside or behind, the two
   blades cross on the ice) and 22 cm across from the hip, a deep edge at once. The entrance is
   7.0 s at 1×, at the crossovers' own speed, not slowed.
5. **The side and rear cameras** take 0.3 s to come round after a step.
6. **Button names**: "Forward inside mohawk", "Backward crossovers and a mohawk", and "Forward
   outside edge" for the waltz jump's and Axel's default.
7. **The waltz jump and Axel share one run** into their identical first key, with a 0.5 s hold
   for the free leg.
