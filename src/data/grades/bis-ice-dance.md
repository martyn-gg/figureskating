Each national ice dance level has a pattern dance test and a free dance test, and a skater can
take the pattern dances up their own ladder without the free dance. This page lists the pattern
dances only. The first three levels name two dances each; from National 4 the skater chooses
two from a list.

Above National 3 the tests split. A skater who wants to compete takes the competitive pattern
dance pathway, where British Ice Skating's ice dance committee sets the dances each
season. The national pathway shown here is the one for skaters testing for themselves.
