British Ice Skating's free skating ladder is Beginner and National 1 to 8. A skater passes a
National level by skating a programme that reaches the technical (TES) and programme
component (PCS) scores set for that level. From National 6 there is a short programme as well as a free one.

The two ladders interlock. To enter a National level a skater needs the Skills test of the
same number and the National level below it, so National 4 needs Skills 4 and National 3.
Beginner needs Skate UK Platinum Star instead.

Each line below is our summary of the 2026-27 criteria, linked to the jumps, spins and turns
in this guide. The criteria say more than these lines carry, from which jumps may repeat to
the spin level features allowed, so read them before a test. They change by season: the
2023 test structure differs from them at National 3 and 4.
