U.S. Figure Skating tests skating skills, the patterns once called moves in the field, at eight
standard levels: Pre-Preliminary, Preliminary, Pre-Bronze, Bronze, Pre-Silver, Silver, Pre-Gold
and Gold. Skate United tests use the same patterns, and adult and adaptive tracks take their own
selections from them. The 2026-27 rulebook sets out every pattern with a diagram and names its
focus: power, edge quality, extension, quickness, or a pair of them.

Each line below is one pattern, by its U.S. name. A pattern is several elements skated in
sequence, so a line can link more than one page, and the links show one foot where the pattern
is skated on both. Where a pattern is built from something the guide has no page for, the line
says so.
