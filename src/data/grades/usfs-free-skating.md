U.S. Figure Skating's singles tests are free skating programmes, judged against a minimum
content for each level: so many jump elements, so many spins, and a step or choreographic
sequence. The levels share their names with the skating skills tests, Pre-Preliminary to Gold,
and adult tests run beside them under their own names.

Each line below is our summary of a requirement in the 2026-27 chart, linked to the jumps and
spins in this guide. The chart says more than these lines carry: which jumps may repeat, how
many combinations are allowed, revolution counts for every spin. Read it before a test.
