Aussie Skate is Ice Skating Australia's learn-to-skate programme. Its instructor's manual
lays out Tiny Tots 1 to 3 for children of five and under, then Basic 1 and 2, Novice 1 and 2
and Intermediate 1 and 2, with free skate, hockey and adult streams after them. The manual
describes how each skill is done and assessed, which is why the guide's basics lean on it.

The level names here are the manual's. In 2021 Ice Skating Australia renamed Basic 1 to
Intermediate 2 as Aussie Skate 1 to 10, and Tiny Tots as Tots; the table that maps old levels
to new is in a 2021 curriculum document that is not published on its site. So the skills
below are in the manual's order and under its level names, and a skater on a numbered Aussie
Skate level should match by skill, not by name.
