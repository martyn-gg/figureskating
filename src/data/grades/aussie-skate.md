Aussie Skate is Ice Skating Australia's learn-to-skate programme. Its instructor's manual
lays out Tiny Tots 1 to 3 for children of five and under, then Basic 1 and 2, Novice 1 and 2
and Intermediate 1 and 2, with free skate, hockey and adult streams after them. The manual
describes how each skill is done and assessed, which is why the guide's basics lean on it.

The level names here are the manual's. From April 2021 Ice Skating Australia calls Basic 1
to Intermediate 2 Aussie Skate 1 to 10, and Tiny Tots plain Tots (its Communication 125).
Basic 1 and 2 became Aussie Skate 1 and 2, and each later level became two: Novice 1 is now
Aussie Skate 3 and 4, and so on up to Intermediate 2 as Aussie Skate 9 and 10. Which skills
went into which half is in a 2021 curriculum document that is not published on its site, so
the skills below stay in the manual's order and a skater on Aussie Skate 5 should look under
Novice 2 and match by skill.
