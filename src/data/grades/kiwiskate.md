KiwiSkate is the New Zealand Ice Figure Skating Association's learn-to-skate programme, a
ladder of badges. Tiny Tots 1 and 2 are for skaters aged six and under; everyone else starts at
Beginner and works up through Elementary, Basic, Novice 1 and 2 to Advanced. Figure, stroking,
free skating and dance badges run beside the main ladder.

Each badge lists skills for assessment, which are what is tested, and teaching progression
ideas, which are taught and not tested. The lists below are the skills for assessment, in the
2025 rules' order and words. The dance badges are left out until the guide holds the pattern
dances.
