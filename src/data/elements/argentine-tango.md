The Argentine Tango is a fast, travelling dance on strong edges, and US Figure Skating asks for real élan with no visible pushing. It opens in open hold with progressive and chassé sequences, then a cross behind with a change of edge on step 9 and a counter for the lead partner on step 10 bring the couple into closed hold.

The rulebook singles out the follow partner's twizzle-like rotation on step 23, skated hip to hip while the lead partner turns a swing open S step, and the closing run of five cross rolls in outside hold, the last held for three beats.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 8, where it is one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at Gold.
