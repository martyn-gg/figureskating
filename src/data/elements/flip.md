Back inside edge, the free toe pick in, vault and rotate. Mechanically the pick action
is identical to a Lutz. The only difference is which edge you are on when you place
the pick, and the rotation goes with the curve rather than against it.

Seen from above the two are immediately distinguishable and from the side they are
nearly identical — which is why the guide shows both views together.

What it rests on is the back inside edge and the three turn that usually arrives on it,
and on setting a pick behind without leaning back onto it. Both are elements here, and a
flip that rotates early is almost always the three turn before it rotating early.
