Any position, basic or not, on one blade or two, for at least three consecutive revolutions,
with changes of foot allowed. The ISU, British Ice Skating and U.S. Figure Skating define it in
the same words, as a spin that serves the choreography of the programme and matches the music.
It has a fixed base value, and the judges' grade of execution is all that moves it.

The ISU's clarifications say more than the rule. The three counting revolutions must be on the
same foot, travelling is allowed, and so is spinning on other parts of the body. Positions used
in it are not counted as used in the other spins.

British Ice Skating asks for it in the National 7 and 8 free skates. The choreographic spinning
movement in ice dance is a different element, and must not meet a dance spin's requirements.
