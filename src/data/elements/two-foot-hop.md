From standing still you bend both knees, spring up off both feet, and come down on both in the
place you left. Ice Skating Australia teaches it at its second level and asks for good posture
on the way up and the arms placed correctly once the blades are back on the ice. The US
Figure Skating adaptive Pre-Bronze test accepts it in place of a bunny hop.

New Zealand's KiwiSkate builds a progression out of it. The jump on the spot is taught at the
Basic badge and a two-foot jump that turns from forwards to backwards at Novice 2. A full turn
in the air on both feet is among the teaching ideas for Free skating 2, the badge that assesses
the half flip.
