The Silver Samba is a quick dance of runs, chassés and slips, and US Figure Skating lets couples add Samba character provided steps, free legs and holds stay intact. It starts in Kilian hold with two runs, each finished by a quick cross-over slip on steps 4 and 8, before the follow partner's swing three-turn takes the couple into closed hold.

The rulebook singles out the lead partner's three-turn on step 26, stepped on count four and turned on count one, the triple swing of the free leg on step 29, and the tuck of the free foot on the two closing inside edges.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 7, where it is one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at International level.
