name: Axel + toe loop
kind: combo
summary: Axel (LFO, off the edge) straight into a toe loop (RBO, off the pick), 2.5 rotations in all.

combo:
  first: axel

  count: 1
verified: { checked: false }

The Axel is the one jump here with a forward takeoff, so the first jump turns the skater
from forwards to backwards. Its extra half turn means the landing has to be checked quickly
to keep the speed.

The Axel lands RBO and the toe loop takes off from RBO:
one edge, held, with no step or turn on it. In a jump sequence the skater may hop or step
between the jumps; in a combination there is nothing between them.

The toe loop is the usual second jump. The landing edge is already its takeoff, so the
free foot reaches back, the toe goes in behind, and the pick gives the lift that the
remaining speed cannot.
