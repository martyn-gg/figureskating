---
name: Flip + loop
kind: combo
summary: Flip (LBI, off the pick) straight into a loop (RBO, off the edge), 2 rotations in all.
aliases: [1F+1Lo]
prerequisites: [flip, loop]
combo:
  first: flip
  second: loop
  count: 1
verified: { checked: false }
---

The flip leaves a back inside edge, vaulting off the toe of the free foot, and lands on the
back outside edge the second jump takes off from.

The flip lands RBO and the loop takes off from RBO:
one edge, held, with no step or turn on it. In a jump sequence the skater may hop or step
between the jumps; in a combination there is nothing between them.

The loop is the harder second jump. With no pick, the skater holds the landing edge, brings
the free leg through to cross in front and springs off the same edge. Its base value is
higher than a toe loop's.
