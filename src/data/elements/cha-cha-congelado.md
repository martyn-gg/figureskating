The Cha Cha Congelado brings a Latin rhythm to dancers early in their development, and US Figure Skating wants that rhythm felt through the body as well as the feet. The opening slip steps give the count of "1, 2, cha-cha-cha", and couples may add their own Cha Cha styling provided steps, free legs and holds stay intact.

The holds change often: outside, both hands joined for a run of crossed touchdown steps, closed, open and Kilian. The rulebook singles out the follow partner's step 5b, a cross behind that many skaters leave out, and the very deep inside edge near the end, where retrogressing on the pattern is allowed.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 8 as one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at International level.
