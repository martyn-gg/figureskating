Both blades stay on the ice and you travel backwards in a zigzag, twisting from the waist so
that the feet swing from side to side under you. Ice Skating Australia asks for about a metre
in six wiggles, with the twist in the upper body while the head and arms stay where they are.
New Zealand's 2008 KiwiSkate badges asked for a distance equal to the skater's height in four
zigzags. Learn to Skate USA asks for six to eight at its first level.

All three programmes teach it before the backward swizzle, which replaces the twist with the
heels pressing out and drawing back in. It is the first time a skater moves backwards on
purpose.
