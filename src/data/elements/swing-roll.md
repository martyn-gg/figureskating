A long edge, held for several beats, during which the free leg swings past the skating foot and
returns beside it for the next step. Going forwards it swings back, then forward, then back
beside; going backwards it swings forward, then back past the skating foot, then beside. The
ISU's wording is that the swing gives the sense of a rolling movement.

U.S. Figure Skating separates it from a plain swing, which stays on the same lobe as the edge
before it. A swing roll starts a new one. Inside edges count too: the Rhythm Blues has forward
inside swing rolls.

KiwiSkate's Dance 1 badge asks for forward outside swing rolls to a count of six, and the Dutch
Waltz, the Fourteenstep and the American Waltz are built on them.
