A step sequence is a run of turns and steps skated as one element across the ice, to the
character of the music. British Ice Skating sorts what goes into it. The simple turn is the
three turn and the simple steps are chassés, mohawks, changes of edge and cross rolls; the
difficult turns are twizzles, brackets, loops, counters and rockers, and the difficult step is
the choctaw. U.S. Figure Skating's list matches it under its own names, C steps and S steps for
mohawks and choctaws, and adds toe steps.

Both want the pattern clear and the ice used: a line, a serpentine, a circle or an oval. The
turns are judged on clean edges, and the technical panel gives a level from basic up to 4 for
features such as difficult turns on both feet. British Ice Skating's free skating tests first
ask for one at National 3.
