Tango Romantica is a soft, lyrical tango, though US Figure Skating still asks for deep edges to carry its mood. It opens with the "Helicopter", a double three-turn for both partners with the free leg lifted to the side, and moves through closed, outside, Kilian, reversed Kilian and open holds.

The rulebook singles out the lead partner's twizzle-like swing on Step 5, timed with the follow partner's open S step, and the matching crossed rockers on Step 22. On Steps 28 and 29 comes the "criss-cross", where the follow partner uses cross rolls to change sides and the pair finish close together on two feet. Late in the pattern the follow partner turns a rocker and a three against the lead partner's swing closed S step.

Tango Romantica is not on the British Ice Skating pattern dance lists. US Figure Skating tests it at International level.
