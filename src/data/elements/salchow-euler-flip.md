The Salchow swings off a back inside edge and lands on the back outside edge like almost
every jump here. Landing it with the knee bent and the free leg held back leaves the speed and the
balance for the next jump.

The Salchow lands RBO and the Euler takes off from it, turns once
and lands LBI on the other foot, where the flip takes off. Nothing comes between
them, and the Euler counts only here, between two jumps, as a single.

The flip here has no turn in front of it. The Euler lands on its takeoff edge, and the
free foot's toe goes in behind straight away.
