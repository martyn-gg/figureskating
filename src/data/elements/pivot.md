One toe pick goes into the ice and stays there. The other foot pushes and scribes a circle
around it, so the skater rotates about a fixed point and does not travel. The tracing is a
circle with a dot at its centre.

The anchoring foot is doing more work than it appears to. The pick has to be set with real
weight through it and the ankle held firm, because a pick that is only resting on the ice
skips and the centre wanders. The circle then comes out as a spiral and the movement stops
being a pivot. The scribing foot rides on an inside edge and keeps pushing without a break,
which is what keeps the rotation smooth.

It is one of the few movements in this guide that use the toe pick to hold position.
Elsewhere the pick leaves the ice.
