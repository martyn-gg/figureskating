Travelling backwards, you push with the inside edge of one blade, glide on the other, and change
feet. Ice Skating Australia describes it: the push comes off the inside edge without the toe
pick, each stroke is held for two to three seconds, and the free foot travels ahead of the skating foot, above the line it is drawing. It asks for at least ten strokes.

New Zealand's KiwiSkate names the shape of the push: a C. Its 2008 badges build it in two
stages, first alternating C sculling with neither foot lifted, eight push-and-glide sequences,
then C pushes pushing and gliding on each foot in turn. The current rules assess the second,
C pushes with the foot lifted. The toe pick is ruled out going backwards as it is going
forwards.
