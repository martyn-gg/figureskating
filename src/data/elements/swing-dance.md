US Figure Skating describes the Swing Dance as teaching a relaxed change from forward to
backward skating, with one partner leading and the other following backwards. Both skate all
thirty steps, a full circuit, but start half a pattern apart: the lead at step 1 and the follow
at step 16. Steps 1 to 8 and 16 to 23 are the same run of chassés and swing rolls, so while one
partner skates it forwards the other skates it backwards.

The hold is closed for those runs. For steps 9 to 13 and 24 to 28 the partners hold hands only,
so the one going backwards can turn and step forwards. Step 13 turns backwards again with a
forward inside C step, in any uncrossed form.

British Ice Skating offers it at National 5 for pattern dance, one of five to choose two from.
KiwiSkate assesses it on the third dance badge.
