The free foot goes down on the outer edge side of the skating foot with the free leg crossed
tightly in front of the skating leg, the legs crossing below the knees. The ISU, British Ice
Skating and U.S. Figure Skating define it in the same words, and the crossed step behind is the
same step crossed behind.

Neither definition names an edge, and the dances use both: a forward outside one opens the
Tango, and backward inside ones turn up in the Fourteenstep and the Fiesta Tango. What does not
count, in the pattern dance handbook's questions and answers, is a foot put down across the
tracing at a distance. The feet need not touch, but should be as close as possible.

KiwiSkate's Dance 4 badge asks for a cross step to a count of two without saying front or
behind. Its dance, the Fiesta Tango, has both.
