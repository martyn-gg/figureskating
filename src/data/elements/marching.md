You walk across the ice, picking each foot up off it in turn. Ice Skating Australia asks for
eight to ten steps without help and makes lifting every foot the one condition. New Zealand's
KiwiSkate teaches marching with high knees and marching round on the spot, and assesses
backward marching at its Beginner badge, with the toes turned in.

Every programme moves straight from the march to a glide. Australia asks for three to five
steps into a two-foot glide of about a metre, and Learn to Skate USA's Snowplow Sam elements
open with a march followed by a two-foot glide and a dip.
