The partners skate apart with arms extended and one pair of hands clasped. Facing the same way,
they travel side by side or one behind the other; the drawing shows them side by side. Facing
opposite ways, one skates forwards and the other backwards with the joined arms out to the
side, and in the Cha Cha Congelado the pair can even be back to back.

U.S. Figure Skating's rulebook counts the arm-in-arm position, side by side with arms linked,
as a variation of it.

The Swing Dance moves into it for its forward steps, steps 9 to 13 and 24 to 28, and asks for
at least 24 inches between the partners there, so both can skate forwards without catching
each other's feet.
