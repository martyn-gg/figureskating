A small circle traced on one foot, on one edge, forwards, coming back to the edge it left
and carrying on. The foot, the edge and the direction all stay the same, and that is what
separates it from a turn.

It is a figure, and it behaves like one: the whole thing is decided by the entry. The loop
should sit inside the lobe, be about the size of the skater's own body, and rejoin the curve
so cleanly that the tracing reads as a single lobe with a knot in it.
