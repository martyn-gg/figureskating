The Tea-Time Foxtrot is a smooth, travelling slow foxtrot, and US Figure Skating's picture of it is a full cup of tea balanced on the follow partner's head with not a drop spilled. It asks for an upright frame with no leaning, light contact in closed hold, slow-quick-quick timing and no break of hold, even through the twizzles.

Most of it is skated in foxtrot hold or a blend of foxtrot and Kilian. The rulebook singles out the toe-pick hops, two inside Ina Bauers (the second turning with a rocker-like motion), a crossed slide into a skidded three-turn, the lead partner's half-rotation jump, a five-beat outside bracket in reverse Kilian, and a closing set of walk-around threes in waltz hold.

Tea-Time Foxtrot is not on the British Ice Skating pattern dance lists. US Figure Skating tests it at International level.
