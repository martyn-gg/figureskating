The free foot crosses in front of the skating foot and goes down on the inside
edge of the other foot, on the same circle. It is how a skater builds speed on a curve, and
it is the first thing after plain edges that most people learn.

The push is the whole point and the part most often thrown away. Both feet push: the outside
foot as it crosses, and then the inside foot as it comes out from under. A crossover done as
a step over, with no push from either foot, looks tidy and produces nothing.
