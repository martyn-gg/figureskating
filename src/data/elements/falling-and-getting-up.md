You sink into a dip, bring the arms in to the chest, and sit down onto the ice to one side of
the bottom, keeping the arms in as you go. To get up you roll forwards onto your hands and
knees, bring one foot up to the ice between your hands, then the other, and stand from there.
Ice Skating Australia and New Zealand's KiwiSkate describe it in nearly the same steps, and
both put it first on their first level.

Australia is specific about the feet coming up: both blades flat on the ice between the hands,
with no toe picks. Its first level has the fall from standing still. At Basic 1 the skater
takes three steps and a glide first, so the fall happens while moving.
