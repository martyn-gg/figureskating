The Viennese Waltz is light and lilting, and US Figure Skating asks for strongly curved edges, soft knees, neat footwork and elegant carriage. Its progressive sequences are skated with the partners slightly offset in a partial outside hold, and their timing breaks the usual waltz count: two one-beat edges, a three-beat edge, then a one-beat cross roll. The rulebook insists this timing be kept.

The changes of edge on steps 5 and 20 are where the partners swap sides, with the free foot passing close to the skating foot. Steps 9 to 11 combine a closed S step for the lead partner with an open C step for the follow, timed two plus one. The pattern closes on a six-beat swing roll in closed hold.

The Viennese Waltz is not on the British Ice Skating national pattern dance lists. US Figure Skating tests it at Gold.
