You skate the length of the rink on one foot, changing from inside to outside and back again,
then change feet at the centre and do it backwards, outside to inside, for the second length.
That is U.S. Figure Skating's Pre-Bronze skating skills element, with power as its focus; its
Adult Silver test asks for the same with continuous flow and strength as the focus.

Each change of edge is the tracing on one foot moving from one curve to a different curve,
which is U.S. Figure Skating's and British Ice Skating's shared definition. A pull is that
change done for speed: the blade driven from one curve into the next.

It is the two-foot change of edge on one foot, and the element before the Pre-Gold power pulls.
