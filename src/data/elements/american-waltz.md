The American Waltz has very few steps: every edge is held for six beats, two full bars, and each lobe of three steps mirrors the one before on the other foot. The rulebook still calls it one of the hardest dances to skate well, because of how much rotation the couple builds up.

US Figure Skating asks the partners to keep their shoulders parallel with the centre of turning between them, so neither one whips round the other on the swing threes. The free leg swings straight from the hip, the threes are turned on count four with the feet close, and a bend of the skating knee marks each count one.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 7, one of five dances to choose from there. US Figure Skating tests it at Silver.
