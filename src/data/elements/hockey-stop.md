Travelling forwards, you turn both feet sideways together, parallel, and skid to a stop. It is a fifth-level skill in Learn to Skate USA. New Zealand's KiwiSkate calls it the forward
two-foot parallel side stop and assesses it both ways, turning to the left and to the right, at
its Novice 2 badge. Ice Skating Australia teaches it in the second level of its hockey stream.

All three name the stop, and none of them, in the documents this guide holds, describes how it
is done: how far the blades tip, where the weight goes, what the upper body does as the feet
turn. Those are a coach's to give. This page says what the stop is and where it sits, and no
more until a published description or a coach fills it in.
