---
name: Closed hold
kind: hold
summary: "Partners face each other, one skating forwards and one backwards, directly opposite with their shoulders parallel."
aliases: ["closed position", "waltz hold", "waltz position"]
sourceUrl: https://usfigureskating.org/documents/2026/8/13/2026-27_Rulebook.pdf

The partners stand directly opposite each other, so one faces the way the couple travels and
skates forwards while the other skates backwards. The lead's right hand sits on the follow's
back at the shoulder blade, with the elbow raised and bent enough to keep the follow close.
The follow's left arm rests along the lead's upper arm, elbow to elbow, with the hand on the
lead's shoulder. The other two arms are extended to the side at shoulder height, hands
joined. The shoulders stay parallel.

U.S. Figure Skating's rulebook also calls it the waltz hold. The Fourteenstep, the American
Waltz, the European Waltz and the Willow Waltz are skated in it nearly throughout, and the
Swing Dance is closed apart from its hand-in-hand steps.

Offset or partial outside versions shift one partner a little to the side, part way to the
outside hold.
