The 14 Step is a brisk march, and US Figure Skating wants it danced, with soft knees in time with the music and a strong lean to reach proper edges at pace. Along the side the couple is in closed hold, the follow partner skating backward, through two progressive sequences either side of a four-beat swing roll with matching free legs.

The ends are where the dance turns over. The lead partner skates an open choctaw at Step 8 and checks rotation with the shoulders, and the couple moves into an offset closed hold. The follow partner then gradually moves ahead for an open choctaw of their own, and closed hold returns at Step 14.

British Ice Skating lists the 14 Step at National 5, as one of the dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it, as the Fourteenstep, at Pre-silver.
