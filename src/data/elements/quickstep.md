The Quickstep is skated in Kilian hold from start to finish, both partners on the same steps, and US Figure Skating stresses that the couple must stay hip to hip throughout. The skating should match its fast, bright music. The pattern runs nearly the length of the rink, so the rulebook asks for a start near the midline at one end to keep the edges true.

After a chassé and a progressive, step 5 opens a closed swing S step, and the backward exit edge then changes from inside to outside with a distinct shift of lean. Steps 7 to 10 are deep crossed edges, and the closing run of steps 13 to 18, including a half-beat change of edge, should be light and well curved.

British Ice Skating lists the Quickstep at National 7, one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at Gold.
