You spin upright on a back edge with the free leg crossed over the skating leg, and leave on
a back outside edge with the free leg extended. Ice Skating Australia asks for three
revolutions at its Freeskate 3 level, on the correct part of the blade; U.S. Figure Skating's
adaptive Silver test asks for four.

None of the documents behind this guide names the foot or the edge it turns on. What they agree
on is that it is a different spin from the forward one: U.S. Figure Skating lets a
Pre-Preliminary skater count both, because a backward upright spin has a different character.
The exit is a back outside edge, the edge every jump in this guide lands on.

New Zealand's KiwiSkate teaches it at Free skating 1 from a pivot entrance and asks for it
again at Free skating 3 with a correct entry.
