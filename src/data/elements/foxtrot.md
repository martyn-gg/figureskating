The Foxtrot is skated on deep edges round semicircular lobes, and US Figure Skating insists it be danced with soft knees and flow, the free foot always set down close beside the skating foot. It opens in open hold with a crossed behind chassé for both, then closes up as the lead partner skates a cross-rolled three-turn and the follow partner holds a four-beat cross roll.

The rulebook singles out the follow partner's cross-rolled three at Step 5, the near outside hold at the end of Step 8 for the lead partner's second cross-rolled three, and the follow partner's closed choctaw on Step 11. On Step 12 both free legs swing up together before two strong inside edges finish the pattern.

British Ice Skating lists the Foxtrot at National 5, as one of the dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at Pre-silver.
