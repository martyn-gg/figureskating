On one foot, travelling backwards, you change edge again and again, each change a pull that
drives the blade into the next curve. U.S. Figure Skating's Pre-Gold skating skills test strings
three power pulls with two quick twisting rockers, repeats the set down the whole diagonal, and
then skates the other diagonal on the other foot, three or four sets a foot. The focus it names
is power and quickness.

No document behind this guide defines a power pull on its own. The meaning here comes from the
test's diagram, which labels each pull as a change of edge, and from the forward and backward
change of edge pulls three levels earlier.

The Gold test uses power pulls again, into and out of double rockers.
