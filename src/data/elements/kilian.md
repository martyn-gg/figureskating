The Kilian is short and fast: fourteen steps to sixteen beats, both partners on the same feet, travelling anticlockwise round an ellipse that can sit anywhere on the ice as long as it stays put once chosen. Only the two outside edges in steps 3 and 4 get two beats, and the rulebook wants them strong and unflattened.

The partners stay pressed together in Kilian hold, and US Figure Skating asks that they never drift apart and back. The rulebook singles out the cross roll and tight cross behind at steps 8 and 9, where the shoulders check to hold the lean, and the crossed open S step into step 10. The clockwise rotation through the back steps must stay controlled.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 7, one of five dances to choose from there. US Figure Skating tests it at Pre-gold.
