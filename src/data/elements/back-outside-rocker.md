name: Back outside rocker
kind: turn
summary: A half turn that holds the edge and hands you a new circle curving the other way.
entry: { foot: R, edge: O, dir: B }
turn: rocker
prerequisites: [forward-outside-three-turn]
verified: { checked: false }
---

A rocker keeps the edge you are on and reverses the direction you are travelling,
which — because those are two of the three things that decide which way a lobe
curves — means the new lobe curves the opposite way. You leave one circle and
join another.

It is worth doing a three turn and a rocker back to back from the same entry edge.
The rotation feels almost identical. What differs is where you end up.
