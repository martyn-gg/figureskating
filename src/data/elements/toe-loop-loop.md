name: Toe loop + loop
kind: combo
summary: Toe loop (RBO, off the pick) straight into a loop (RBO, off the edge), 2 rotations in all.
aliases: [1T+1Lo]

combo:
  first: toeLoop

  count: 1

The toe loop lands on the edge it took off from, so the free foot reaches back and the toe
goes in again almost at once, with no time to settle between the two.

The toe loop lands RBO and the loop takes off from RBO:
one edge, held, with no step or turn on it. In a jump sequence the skater may hop or step
between the jumps; in a combination there is nothing between them.

The loop is the harder second jump. With no pick, the skater holds the landing edge, brings
the free leg through to cross in front and springs off the same edge. Its base value is
higher than a toe loop's.
