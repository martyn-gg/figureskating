The free leg extends behind and the body folds forward over the skating leg to counter it.
What the ISU asks for is the free knee above hip level, which is worth knowing: the
definition is a knee height. Most people picture a flat horizontal line.

It is the slowest spin there is, and for a reason you can see. You are stretched
along a line at right angles to the one you are turning about, so the shoulders
and the free foot each travel a wide circle every revolution while the hip
barely moves. Reaching further out slows it further still, and drawing the arms
in is most of what you have left to speed it up with.

A camel a little off centre does not wobble on the spot. It travels across the ice, and that
is why this is the position where centring gets learned.
