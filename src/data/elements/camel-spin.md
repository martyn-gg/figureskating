The free leg extends behind and the body folds forward over the skating leg to
counter it. What the ISU asks for is the free knee above hip level, which is
worth knowing: the definition is a knee height and not the flat horizontal line
most people picture.

It is the slowest spin there is, and for a reason you can see. You are stretched
along a line at right angles to the one you are turning about, so the shoulders
and the free foot each travel a wide circle every revolution while the hip
barely moves. Reaching further out slows it further still, and drawing the arms
in is most of what you have left to speed it up with.

A camel a little off centre travels across the ice rather than wobbling on the
spot, and that is why this is the position where centring gets learned.
