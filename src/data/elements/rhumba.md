The Rhumba is lively yet soft, and US Figure Skating wants it skated with hidden power and control. Both partners skate the same steps in Kilian hold. Step 3 is a double change of edge, outside to inside and back to outside, with a lift of the free leg on the third beat.

The rulebook singles out the wide steps on 8 and 11, which must be stepped wide and never jumped, and the pair of S steps that follow: an open one crossed in front, then a closed one crossed behind, each finishing on a full four-beat edge. Steps 10 to 14 each start on the third beat of the bar. The dance should stay smooth, without bounce.

British Ice Skating lists the Rhumba at National 8, one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at International.
