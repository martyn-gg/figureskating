---
name: Outside hold
kind: hold
summary: "Partners face opposite ways like the closed hold, offset side by side so they pass hip to hip."
aliases: ["tango hold", "tango position", "outside position"]
sourceUrl: https://usfigureskating.org/documents/2026/8/13/2026-27_Rulebook.pdf
verified: { checked: false }
---

As in the closed hold, one partner skates forwards and the other backwards. The difference is
the offset: the lead stands to one side of the follow, so the front of the lead's hip lines up
with the front of the follow's matching hip. The arms are those of the closed hold, the lead's
right hand on the follow's back and the other hands joined.

U.S. Figure Skating's rulebook also calls it the tango hold, and warns that a tight hip to hip
position is a fault because it stops the couple travelling. The partners should be close but
free to move.

The Tango and the Paso Doble open in it, and the Blues and the Argentine Tango move into it
from the closed hold. A reverse outside hold puts the lead on the other side.
