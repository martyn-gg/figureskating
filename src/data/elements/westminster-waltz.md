The Westminster Waltz is stately, and US Figure Skating asks for an upright stance that never breaks at the waist, with strong edges and softly flowing knees. It begins in Kilian hold, and an inside open C step on steps 5 and 6 brings both partners round individually so they finish in reversed Kilian.

The rulebook singles out step 13, where the follow partner holds a swing for six beats before an inside forward rocker while the lead partner turns a forward outside counter on the same count, hip to hip. Later the follow partner turns a three towards the lead partner's shoulder and is guided across in front on the final swing roll, ending in Kilian hold for the restart.

The Westminster Waltz is not on the British Ice Skating national pattern dance lists. US Figure Skating tests it at Gold.
