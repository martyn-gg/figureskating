Backwards, the free foot set down beside the skating foot with nothing crossing. Quieter
than a crossover and less powerful, which is the trade: a chassé keeps a curve going without
disturbing it.

Because there is no crossing action to hide behind, a chassé shows up a weak edge at once.
If the lobe opens out as the feet change, the edge was never deep.
