The Paso Doble draws on bullring music, and US Figure Skating asks for strong body control and precise footwork. It opens in outside hold with a progressive and two chassés, then reaches its oddest moment: steps 8 and 9 are slip steps, skated on the flat with both blades on the ice while the free foot slides past to full extension, knees bent or straight.

The rulebook singles out the change of edge on step 11, which should push the pattern into a clear outward bulge, and calls the cross rolls on steps 26 to 28 the hardest part of the dance, since deep edges need supple knees at this tempo. The follow partner restarts with a quick open C step on the "and" count.

British Ice Skating lists the Paso Doble at National 8, one of five dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at Pre-gold.
