The Hickory Hoedown takes its music from square dancing, and US Figure Skating wants it light, carefree and full of country-western flair. Partners choose their position for the first seventeen steps, then close up for the run round the end.

The pattern is largely symmetrical: two quick chassés, a progressive and a slide chassé off a cross roll, then the same sequence the other way. The rulebook singles out the three-beat slides at Steps 11 and 16, where the free leg reaches forward and then folds so the foot travels back to the skating knee. Round the end the follow partner skates a three-turn at once, while the lead partner turns after a four-beat swing.

British Ice Skating lists the Hickory Hoedown at National 4, where it is one of the dances to choose from. US Figure Skating tests it at Bronze.
