The hands and arms are where they are in the closed hold: the lead's right hand on the
follow's back, the follow's left hand on the lead's shoulder, the other two hands joined. The
partners turn a little away from each other, so both face the same way and both skate
forwards, side by side, with the joined hands ahead of them.

U.S. Figure Skating's rulebook also calls it the foxtrot hold, and gives a crossed version:
the lead's right arm passes behind the follow to the follow's right hip, and the follow's left
arm passes behind the lead to the lead's left hip.

The Foxtrot, the Rocker Foxtrot and the Argentine Tango all begin in it, and the Foxtrot and
the Rocker Foxtrot come back to it after a stretch in the closed hold.
