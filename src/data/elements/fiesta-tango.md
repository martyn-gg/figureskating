Both partners skate the same sixteen steps. They start in reversed Kilian hold and change to
Kilian at step 11, where an open C step turns them backwards, and they keep that hold until the
dance starts again. The tango here is slow, and US Figure Skating asks for upright carriage, soft
knees and leg swings that match.

Step 8 is the one with room in it. It starts as an open stroke on the right forward outside
edge, the free leg swings on the third count, and from then on the free leg is the skater's to
use, as long as the change to the inside edge comes exactly on its beat, four beats in.

British Ice Skating sets it at National 3 for pattern dance, beside the Festival Cha Cha. New
Zealand's KiwiSkate assesses it on the fourth dance badge. US Figure Skating calls it the Tango
Fiesta.
