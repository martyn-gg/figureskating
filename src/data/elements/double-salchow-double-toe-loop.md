The Salchow swings off a back inside edge and lands on the back outside edge like almost
every jump here. Landing it with the knee bent and the free leg held back leaves the speed and the
balance for the next jump.

The Double Salchow lands RBO and the double toe loop takes off from RBO:
one edge, held, with no step or turn on it. In a jump sequence the skater may hop or step
between the jumps; in a combination there is nothing between them.

The toe loop is the usual second jump. The landing edge is already its takeoff, so the
free foot reaches back, the toe goes in behind, and the pick gives the lift that the
remaining speed cannot.

At double the margin is small: the first jump has to leave enough speed for the second to
find two full turns, and a second jump that comes up short is marked as under-rotated.
