The waltz jump is often the first jump a skater puts in front of another. Its half turn
lands on the same back outside edge as almost every jump here, an edge the skater has already
practised.

The waltz jump lands RBO and the Euler takes off from it, turns once
and lands LBI on the other foot, where the Salchow takes off. Nothing comes between
them, and the Euler counts only here, between two jumps, as a single.

The Salchow here has no three turn in front of it. The Euler has already put the
skater on the back inside edge, so the free leg swings through and the jump goes up from the
landing.
