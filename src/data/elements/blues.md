The Blues asks for strong edges and deep knees from the opening cross rolls, forward for the lead partner and backward for the follow partner. On step 4 the lead partner skates a cross-rolled three-turn towards the side barrier while the follow partner crosses behind then in front, and the couple goes from outside to open hold.

The rulebook singles out the four-beat back outside edge that both partners skate at step 5, which starts on the third beat of the bar, and the promenade of steps 8 to 11, a double progressive where the first and last steps get two beats and the middle two get one.

British Ice Skating lists it at National 6, one of three dances to choose from at that level. US Figure Skating tests it at Pre-gold.
