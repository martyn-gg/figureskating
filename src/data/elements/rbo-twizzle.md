Backwards on an outside edge, one rotation, out where it started. This is the twizzle of ice
dance (done in sequence, in pairs, at speed), and the one most likely to be asked for with
something else immediately after it.

Blind, so it runs off the edge and the shoulders, with nothing you can watch. The exit is
the whole element: the rotation has to stop on the edge it started on, checked. Stopping
two-thirds of the way round and putting in a step to tidy up what is left is the fault.
