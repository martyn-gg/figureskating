The Willow Waltz packs a spread of turns and step sequences into one pattern, and US Figure Skating treats it as a test of clean waltz timing. Its progressives and chassés should be distinct, counted 2-1-3 across each sequence, with soft knees and a gliding feel.

Each partner has one step with a choice in it: Step 6 for the follow partner and Step 19 for the lead partner may be an open stroke with free legs in counter position or a slide chassé with matching free legs. The rulebook also asks for upright carriage and unison, the couple close together with neat footwork and pace that comes without visible pushing.

British Ice Skating lists the Willow Waltz at National 5, as one of the dances to choose from. It is on the KiwiSkate Dance 6 badge in New Zealand, and US Figure Skating tests it at Bronze.
