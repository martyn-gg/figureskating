Facing backwards, you take off from a back edge with the other foot's toe pick, turn half way
in the air toward the picking toe, land on the opposite toe and push off at once onto the other
foot. Ice Skating Australia teaches it at Intermediate 2, from each foot, and leaves the edge
to the skater. Its manual also calls it the toe to toe jump.

Australia's text makes it a jump: the backward take-off and the half rotation put it beside the
half flip. Two details differ from the half flip in the same manual: the turn goes toward the
picking toe where the half flip turns away from it, and the landing pushes straight off onto
the other foot where the half flip holds an edge.
