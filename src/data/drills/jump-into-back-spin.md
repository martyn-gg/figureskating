You take off forwards as for a waltz jump or a single Axel, and instead of landing and gliding
out, you land with the free leg already crossed and keep turning in a back spin. The walkthrough
is the same without leaving the ice: a step from the take-off onto the other foot and one
turn of back spin.

It trains the position the Axel needs in the air: the weight over the landing side and the
legs crossed, so the rotation has an axis to turn about. A skater who lands wide or with the
free leg swinging cannot turn it into a spin, which makes the drill its own check.

It is a later drill, for a skater who already has the waltz jump and the back spin.
