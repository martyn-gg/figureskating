On one half circle: strokes forward onto the inside edge, an open mohawk to go backwards,
a step down onto the other foot, a cross behind and a step forward onto the inside edge to
start again. The next half circle curves the other way on the other feet.

It joins going backwards and coming forwards into one run, which is the footwork in front of
several jump entries. Coaches look for the mohawk's heel to come in to the instep, for the
hips to open evenly, and for each step to land on the curve rather than across it.

At learn-to-skate level it is often the first time a skater strings turns and steps together
without stopping.
