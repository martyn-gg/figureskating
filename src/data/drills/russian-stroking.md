A lap of the rink built from the pieces a skater already has: strokes, crossovers, three
turns, double threes and mohawks, joined without stopping. Each lap adds something, so by the
end the skater is turning and changing direction continuously at speed.

It is a warm-up, and it is also a test of how well the pieces join. A skater who can do each
turn alone often stops between them; the drill finds those stops and asks for them to go.

Because every coach writes their own version, this page does not give a chart. Take the
idea from here and the sequence from whoever sets it.
