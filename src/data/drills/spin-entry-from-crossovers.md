Back crossovers round a circle build speed, then a step forward onto the forward outside edge
sets up the spin, the arms wound back ready to come round. The same circle is skated again and
again so the entry becomes one movement.

Most spins that travel or wobble go wrong before they start, on the entry edge. The drill
repeats the part that decides the spin: the step forward landing on the circle, the edge
held long enough to turn into the centre, and the arms and free leg coming round together.

Coaches set it on the circle a skater will use in a programme, so the entry is practised where
it will be used.
