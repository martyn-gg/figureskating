The three turn done very slowly, exactly half way round each lobe, with the free foot drawn in
beside the skating foot before the turn and again after it. Nothing is hurried: the turn is
placed, then the lobe is finished on the back edge.

Taking the speed away shows whether the turn comes from the body or from momentum. Done
slowly, the shoulders, hips and knee have to do it in the right order, and a turn that slides
or scrapes shows which one was early.

The hopped version adds a small hop through the turn, landing on the same foot. It is how some
coaches first teach the half turn in the air of a waltz jump.
