This is the forward drill run backwards. A back outside three brings you forwards, a mohawk
takes you backwards again onto the other foot, and a back crossover puts you on the back
outside edge for the next three.

Facing forwards after a back three is where control goes: the rotation wants to continue,
and the mohawk has to be ready before it can. Coaches look for the mohawk's free foot to come
in close and the hips to open evenly rather than swing.

Three to five sets fit on one circle of a figure eight before the pattern changes to the
other circle and the other direction.
