Slow, full swizzles, then two-foot slaloms, then pulls on both feet round a circle that switches
direction, one foot on an outside edge and the other on an inside. Each piece is a basic, but
done slowly and fully they wake up the ankles and the knees.

The two-foot pulls are the useful surprise. With both feet on the ice the skater can feel the
edges change together, and switching the circle asks for the change to come from the body
moving over the blades rather than from the feet.

It is a warm-up, so it should be gentle enough to do without thinking and long enough to get
warm.
