A spin position has to be found quickly and then held while the body turns several
times a second. The sit spin needs a skating leg strong enough to hold a deep bend with
the free leg out in front; the camel needs the body and free leg level and still; the
layback needs a back that can arch without collapsing.

These shapes can be held off the ice, still, for longer than a spin lasts. Holding them
still is harder than it sounds and is most of the work: on the ice the rotation adds
pull, but the shape is the same shape.

Flexibility for the layback and the camel is worth building slowly and with advice. A
physiotherapist can say how far your back and hips should go, and that answer differs
from one skater to the next.
