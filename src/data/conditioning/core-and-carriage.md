Carriage is how the upper body is held over the blade: tall, with the shoulders level
and the arms where the element wants them. Turns and jumps both depend on the upper and
lower body moving separately, the shoulders checking one way while the hips turn the
other, and that needs a trunk strong enough to hold its own shape.

Off the ice that is core strength in the broadest sense, front, back and sides, held while
the arms and legs move. Holding still matters more than speed.

Ask a coach to watch your carriage on the floor. Most skaters lean further than they
think, and it is easier to see when nothing is moving under them.
