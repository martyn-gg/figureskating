A free programme lasts a few minutes, a pattern dance several circuits, a step sequence
the length of the rink. The edges and turns in the last third of any of them are skated
by a tired body, and tired skaters straighten their knees and lose their edges first.

Ordinary endurance work does the job: anything that keeps a skater working steadily for
longer than the programme lasts. Stroking laps on the ice count too, and are the most
specific of all.

There is nothing skating-specific about building stamina, so this entry says only why it
matters. Plenty of good general advice exists elsewhere, and a skater with a health
condition should ask a doctor before starting.
