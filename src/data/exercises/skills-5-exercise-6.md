New on 01/10/2026, and the document says outright that the pattern is a figure of eight like
the old school figures. That is a striking thing to find in a modern syllabus and it changes
what the exercise measures: on a figure, the ice keeps a record. The tracing is there to be
looked at afterwards, and a turn that was steered rather than turned leaves a mark that says
so.

Starting from standing removes the last excuse. Nothing carries the turn except what the
body does over a slow edge, and a bracket at low speed is unforgiving in a way it is not at
pace — everything happens slowly enough to be seen.

Two circles on each foot means four brackets on a shape that has to close. Getting back to
where you started is not decoration; it is the check on whether the lobes were equal, and it
is the part of old figures worth keeping.
