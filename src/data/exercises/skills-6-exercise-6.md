Two turns that both fight the curve, one after the other on the same foot. The bracket turns
against the lobe and changes edge; the counter turns against it and does not. Putting them in
one cluster means the skater rotates against the curve twice with a single edge in between to
do the resetting on.

Speed is the thing to get right and the document says so twice, once for each turn. Neither
of these can be forced round, and the edge that joins them is short — arrive fast and the
bracket rotates late, which leaves the counter starting from a lobe that is already wrong.
The skaters who make this look calm are the ones who gave up some speed on the entry.

One note on the source. The October document writes the intermediate edge of the second
cluster as a right back outside edge, which cannot be right: a bracket stays on the same foot,
so a left forward inside entry can only produce a left back outside edge. Skills 7 builds the
same cluster and writes the mirror correctly, so this looks like a transposition rather than
anything intended.
