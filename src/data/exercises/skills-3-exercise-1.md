Skills 1 and 2 asked for changes of edge in a rhythm, one after another down the rink. This
asks for one, and then for half a circle of doing nothing with it. That is a harder demand
than it sounds and a completely different one: a slalom hides an imperfect change inside the
next change, and a held edge has nowhere to put it.

The chassé is what makes the entry honest. Bringing the free foot down beside the skating
foot and taking it up again gives the skater no push to steer with, so the edge that follows
is whatever the lean and the knee actually produced. Open means the free foot returns behind
rather than in front, and this guide keeps that as one element with the closed version,
because the difference is in the free leg rather than in the tracing.

Where the edge comes from changes on the second side. It now arrives after a crossover
rather than after a chassé, with the feet already crossed and the body rotated. Same
element, less time to organise it, and it is the side that shows whether the first one was
being helped along.
