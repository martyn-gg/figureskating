The same exercise as Skills 5's inside brackets with the edge swapped, and the swap is worth
more than it looks. An outside edge carries more speed and rotates more willingly, which
sounds like help and is not: a bracket has to turn against the lobe, so the extra momentum is
momentum in the wrong direction.

The backward outside bracket is the hardest single turn in the first seven tests. The entry is a crossover, the edge behind the skater gives the
least to check against, it turns against everything the lobe is doing, and it changes edge on
the way through. There is
nowhere in it to hide a correction.

The shape is left open, figure of eight or circular, and the choice says something about the
skater. A figure of eight closes and therefore keeps a record of whether the lobes matched; a
circular pattern is more forgiving and looks better skated fast. Four to six brackets either
way, on both feet, with equal quality named in the objectives as the thing being looked for.
