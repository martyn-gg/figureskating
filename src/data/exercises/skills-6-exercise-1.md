Rockers backwards, which means the turn now happens where the skater cannot watch the lobe
they are turning onto. Skills 5 did them forwards with crossovers in between; this pairs each
one with a mohawk immediately after it, so the exercise never contains a finished turn.

That pairing is the interesting part. A rocker keeps its edge and changes lobe, and the
mohawk that follows changes foot on a curve that is already the new one. Nothing about it is
difficult to describe and it goes wrong constantly: the rocker gets cut short so that
the mohawk can be placed, and the lobe that should have been produced by the turn is made by
the step instead.

Each side uses a different mohawk, open on one and closed on the other, which changes where
the free foot goes and not much else about the tracing. Skaters usually find one of the two
sides much stronger, and the exercise is arranged so that showing only that one is not
possible.
