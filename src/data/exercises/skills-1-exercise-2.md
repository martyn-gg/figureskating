Crossovers to build the speed, then one edge held long enough that nothing else can be
holding you up. The position is the first thing in the syllabus with a duration attached
to it: a third of a circle, or three seconds, with the free leg extended and the free foot
turned out.

Both are needed because the exercise runs on both circles. Skating it one way round only
is the commonest way of arriving at Skills 5 with one good side and one that has never
been asked to do anything.

Three seconds on a back outside edge is longer than it sounds. The free leg is behind you
where you cannot see it, the turnout has to come from the hip rather than the knee, and the
whole thing is happening over an edge you are travelling away from.
