The first element in the guide judged on the body rather than on the blade. A spiral is a
held edge with the free leg raised behind, and what is being assessed is the position and
whether it is the same on both feet — which it almost never is, because one hip opens more
easily than the other and three seconds is long enough for that to show.

Everything before the spiral exists to arrive at it with speed and in the right place. The
closed mohawk turns the skater backwards, the crossover carries the lobe, and the whole
thing runs as a serpentine so the exercise crosses the ice rather than circling on it. A
spiral entered slowly is a spiral held on a dying edge, and the position gets blamed for
what the entry did.

Until 01/10/2026 the way in is a toe-assisted hop, which is the one requirement in the first
three tests that asks the skater to leave the ice. From that date it is a choice, and the
alternative reaches the same spiral off an inside edge with both blades staying down.
