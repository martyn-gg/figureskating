The Skills 3 exercise, backwards. That sounds like a small step and it is not: forwards, a
change of edge is watched over the toe and the skater can feel the new lobe arrive under the
ball of the foot; backwards, the same roll happens behind the body's line and the only
information is the pressure on the blade.

Half a circle of holding it is what makes this hard to fake. A change of edge that the
skater is still finishing when the lobe ends can pass in a slalom; here the new edge has to
be established quickly enough to be held, and the shoulders have to stay where they were
put. Rotating with the change is the standard fault and it shows up as a lobe that shrinks.

The second side arrives via a forward 3-turn rather than a crossover, which is the real
difference between the two sides. A skater comes out of that turn with rotation to spare,
and the exercise then asks them to change edge and hold it — so the check has to happen
before the change, not during it.
