The second foot is gone. Skills 1's slalom allowed the change to be shared between two
blades for most of its length, and this exercise removes that everywhere: forwards on each
foot, backwards on each foot, a minimum of two repetitions on each.

Each foot runs inside to outside and back, so the change happens twice on every repetition
and in both directions. Those two are not the same thing to skate. Rolling from an inside
edge to an outside one puts the body over the top of the blade and it will get there on
its own if the knee allows it; coming back is a move you have to make, and it is the one
that arrives late.

Backwards on one foot is the hard end of Skills 2 and possibly of the first three tests.
The knee has to keep its rhythm while the hip is behind the skater, and the usual failure
is not a wrong edge but a slalom that quietly stops travelling — the curves get shallower,
the knee stops working, and the exercise turns into a straight line with a wobble in it.
