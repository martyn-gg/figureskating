The document is unusually direct about the purpose: two difficult turns, combined. A
backward inside counter and a multi-rotation twizzle are both turns that have to be produced
by the body against an edge that offers no help, and here the second one starts from what
the counter leaves behind.

The counter comes first and it is entered from a crossover, so the strong backward inside
edge the objective asks for has to be made in about a second. Everything after that follows:
a counter on a shallow edge cannot be checked, and a twizzle entered without a check
unwinds.

Controlling the exit is the third objective, and it decides whether the rotation ends where
the skater meant it to. Two and a half rotations reverses the direction of travel, so the
skater finishes facing the opposite way to where the counter left them — which is the point
of the count, and why it is that count rather than two or three.
