Three changes of direction in a row, and the only one of Skills 7's clusters that this guide
holds as a single element. The choctaw takes the skater backwards onto the other foot, the 3-turn brings them forwards
again on that foot, and the rocker turns once more and moves the lobe.
By the end of it the skater has turned three times and stepped once.

Speed control is what the objectives ask for, twice. A cluster this long cannot be entered
fast: the rocker at the end has to have a real lobe to turn on, and there will not be one if
the first two turns have been rushed. The instinct to attack it is exactly wrong, and it is
the commonest reason the rocker comes out flat.

Between the clusters the exercise is nothing but crossovers and a step, which is how the
speed is meant to be rebuilt. Four to six clusters down both lengths of the ice means the
last one is skated tired, and that is deliberate — this is the exercise immediately before
Skills 8, where all of it has to happen to music.
