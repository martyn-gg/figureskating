The first test with a rink requirement attached to it. Skills 5 and everything above it can
only be taken on a pad of 40 metres or more, which tells you what changes here: the
exercises need room, and several of them are assessed on whether they use it.

The bracket arrives. It turns against the curve while changing edge, which makes it the turn
with nothing helping: the rotation and the lobe disagree from beginning to end. Before
01/10/2026 the brackets were inside ones only, and the counter waited until Skills 6.

From 01/10/2026 two exercises are added. One is a figure of eight of forward outside
brackets from standing, a turn the previous syllabus held back until Skills 7. The other is
a backward counter exercise, which brings the counter down a level and makes Skills 5 the
test that completes the four one-foot turns.

The triple 3-turn in exercise 1 and the slip chassé in exercise 3 both have pages of their
own. The slip chassé's page shows its tracing but no figure.
