The first test with a rink requirement attached to it. Skills 5 and everything above it can
only be taken on a pad of 40 metres or more, which tells you what changes here: the exercises
need room, and several of them are assessed on whether they use it.

The bracket arrives, and with it the last of the four one-foot turns. A bracket turns against
the curve while changing edge, which makes it the turn with nothing helping — the rotation
and the lobe disagree from beginning to end. From 01/10/2026 there is a second bracket
exercise skated as a figure of eight from standing, and a counter exercise after it.

This is also where the syllabus starts asking for things this guide cannot draw. The slip
chassé in exercise 3 puts both blades flat on the ice, which the rig has no way to hold, and
the triple 3-turn in exercise 1 is one more repetition than the model has an element for.
Both are named on the exercises that ask for them.
