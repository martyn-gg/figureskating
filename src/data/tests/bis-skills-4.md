The turn family opens up here. Everything below Skills 4 is built from 3-turns and mohawks,
and this test adds the rocker and the choctaw — the first turn that holds its edge through
the rotation, and the first change of foot that changes the curve as well.

It is also the level where turns stop arriving one at a time. Double 3-turns, a choctaw with
a mohawk immediately after it, a twizzle rolled straight into a cross roll: the exercises are
built so that nothing gets a clean landing to settle on.

The two exercises added on 01/10/2026 are both about running things together rather than
about anything new. One alternates mohawks with 3-turns, the other puts a double 3-turn into
a cross roll, and neither introduces an element the test did not already contain.
