The first of the eight National Skills tests, and the one that decides whether everything
above it is built on anything. Its subject is the edge: four forward edges, four
backward ones, and the pushes and steps between them.

The skater has to turn backwards before the extended position, and the test leaves the
method to them: a three turn or a mohawk, their choice, with an optional turn of the same
kind halfway through the crossovers. Until 01/10/2026 that is all the turning Skills 1
asks for. From that date a fifth exercise makes forward outside and forward inside three
turns compulsory, which brings the first required turn in the syllabus down from Skills 2.
The edge still comes first: each outside three turn exits onto the back outside edge that
the second exercise has already asked the skater to hold.

What the test is really asking is whether the skater can hold a curve without help.
A sustained edge, an extended position held for a third of a circle, a slalom that keeps
its speed: each of these fails the moment the skater's weight is not over the biting edge,
and none of them can be faked with effort.
