The first of the eight National Skills tests, and the one that decides whether everything
above it is built on anything. Its whole subject is the edge: four forward edges, four
backward ones, and the pushes and steps between them.

Nothing here turns except by choice. The one direction change in Skills 1 is optional and
the skater picks how to make it, which is a deliberate ordering — a three turn learned
before the edge it turns on is a three turn learned twice.

What the test is really asking is whether the skater can hold a curve without help.
A sustained edge, an extended position held for a third of a circle, a slalom that keeps
its speed: each of these fails the moment the skater's weight is not over the biting edge,
and none of them can be faked with effort.
