Skills 8 is three sections skated in order and linked into a single programme to music of no
more than two minutes thirty, with the start, the finish and the material between the
sections left to the skater. It is the only test in the National Skills structure that is
choreographed. Every other test is a set of exercises.

The assessment changes with it. Musicality is a sixth pillar here and nowhere else, so
a turn that is clean and lands off the beat is not the same as a turn that is clean. Apart from
the double twizzle, every turn in the three sections is one the skater has met by Skills 7,
and the difference is that they now have to arrive somewhere in particular.

It is the top of the ladder for a competitor as well as for a tester. Junior and Senior both
ask for Skills 8 to enter a national championship, under the
[2026/27 requirements](https://www.iceskating.org.uk/post/updates-to-championship-requirements-for-the-2026-27-season),
with a National 7 or National 8 in technical and components alongside. Nothing above this
level is published outside a member login, so nothing above it is covered here.

Two things in it sit outside this guide: the pivot movement in section 1, and from
01/10/2026 the push or touch down before the double twizzle in section 2. Both are named on
the sections that ask for them. The same revision publishes section 2 in two versions, 2A
and 2B, identical until the double twizzle, which 2A takes on the foot the section is
already on and 2B on the other. The steps crossed behind, which appear at no other level,
have had a page since 30/08/2026.
