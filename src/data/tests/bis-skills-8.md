Not a set of exercises. Skills 8 is three sections skated in order and linked into a single
programme to music of no more than two minutes thirty, with the start, the finish and the
material between the sections left to the skater. It is the only test in the National Skills
structure that is choreographed rather than set.

The assessment changes with it. Musicality is a sixth pillar here and nowhere else, so
a turn that is clean and lands off the beat is not the same as a turn that is clean. Almost
every element in the three sections has been in the syllabus since Skills 5 or 6, and the
difference is that they now have to arrive somewhere in particular.

It is the top of the ladder for a competitor as well as for a tester. Junior and Senior both
ask for Skills 8 to enter a national championship, under the
[2026/27 requirements](https://www.iceskating.org.uk/post/updates-to-championship-requirements-for-the-2026-27-season),
with a National 7 or National 8 in technical and components alongside. Nothing above this
level is published outside a member login, so nothing above it is covered here.

Two things in it sit outside this guide. The pivot movement in section 1 and the push or
touch down before the double twizzle in section 2 both put something on the ice that the
model does not hold. Both are named on the sections that ask for them. The steps crossed
behind, which appear at no other level, have had a page since 30/08/2026.
